LOOSE STOOLS/DIARRHEA
This information is for educational purposes.

Treatment

  • CAP.RACECADOTRIL/ZEDOTT 100 mg TDS (1.5mg/kg/dose in children) (OR)
    • RACECADOTRIL sachet 10/15/30 mg TDS
  • T NUTROLIN B/SPOROLAC DS/ C VIZYLAC/C DAROLAC(LACTOBACILLUS COMBINATIONS) 1-1-1
  • Abdominal pain- TAB.DICYCLOMINE+PARACETAMOL/CYCLOPAM (OR) TAB.BUSCOPAN/HYOSCINE BUTYLBROMIDE 1 SOS
  • ORS in small sips
    • 4.3g packet is mixed with 200ml
    • 21g packet mixed with 1L in water which is first boiled and then cooled
    • Dosage after each purge
      • <6months :50 ml or 1/4 glass
      • 6months-2years: 50-100ml(1/4-1/2glass)
      • 2years-5 years:100-200 ml(1/2-1 glass)

      • 5years:as much as able to drink

    • If child vomits,wait for 10 min & then resume feeding
  • Give plenty of oral fluids
  • For childrens give ZINC(0.5mg/kg/day) (OR)
    • 10 mg daily for age 2-6 months
    • 20 mgf or >6months
    • Below 2 months not indicated
  • VERY SEVERE- TAB.LOPERAMIDE/IMODIUM / LOPAMIDE 2mg 2tabs STAT and then 1 after each episode
    • (C/I in <4 yrs and in acute infective diarrhoea and pregnancy)

General Treatment in Adults

  • Watery stools
    • TAB.CIPROFLOXACIN+TINIDAZOLE/ CIPLOX-TZ (OR) TAB.OFLOXACIN+ORNIDAZOLE/ZENFLOX-OZ 1-0-1 x 5 days
  • CAP.RACECADOTRIL/ZEDOTT 100 mg TDS
    Colicky pain- TAB.DICYCLOMINE+PARACETAMOL/CYCLOPAM (OR) TAB.BUSCOPAN/HYOSCINE BUTYLBROMIDE 1TDS
  • ORS
  • Water/fluids/ganji
  • T NUTROLIN B/SPOROLAC DS/ C VIZYLAC/C DAROLAC(LACTOBACILLUS COMBINATIONS) 1-1-1 (OR) SYP\C.BACILLUS CLAUSII /ENTEROGERMINA (probiotic)
    • ENTEROGERMINA Dose
      • adults: 1 Capsule bd/dtds;
      • children:1 capsule od or bd or Syp 5ml bd,
      • breast feeding infants 5 ml od or bd for 2-5days
  • SEVERE- INJ.GENTAMYCIN 80mg IM/IV q 8hr
  • RL & DNS(2-10 bottles)

General Treatment in Infants upto 1 year old

  • SYP.NALIDIXIC ACID/GRAMONEG 10 drops TDS
  • Watery stools- ORALYTE feeds 50ml
  • Crying-SYP.DICYCLOMINE+DIMETHICONE/SPASMINDON 5 drops
  • Vomiting- SYP.ONDANSETRON/ONDEM 5-10 drops
